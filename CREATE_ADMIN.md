# Create Admin User - Step by Step Guide

## Open a NEW terminal (don't close the server) and run:

```powershell
C:/Users/ritikg/Desktop/Projects/ritik123gaire.github.io/.venv/Scripts/python.exe manage.py createsuperuser
```

## You'll be asked for:
1. Username: (choose any username)
2. Email address: (your email - can be left blank)
3. Password: (choose a strong password)
4. Password (again): (confirm your password)

## After creating the superuser:
1. Go to: http://127.0.0.1:8000/admin/
2. Login with your username and password
3. Start managing your portfolio content!

## What You Can Do in Admin:
- ✏️ Edit your profile, bio, and social links
- 📚 Add/edit education and experience
- 💻 Manage skills by category
- 🎨 Add/edit services you offer
- 🚀 Upload and showcase projects with images
- 📝 Write and publish blog posts
- 📊 Update statistics/achievements
- 📧 View contact form messages

## Your website is at:
http://127.0.0.1:8000/

All changes made in the admin panel will appear on your website immediately!
